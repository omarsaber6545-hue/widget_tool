Discord Account Token

To broadcast your presence 24/7 in the cloud without keeping your PC on, CustomRP Web connects to Discord Gateway using your user token.

Token is stored locally on this machine/server only.
How to get your token in 10 seconds:
  1. Open discord.com/app in Chrome/Brave.
  2. Press F12 > Console.
  3. Paste this snippet and press Enter:
window.webpackChunkdiscord_app.push([[Math.random()],{},(req)=>{for(const m of Object.keys(req.c).map(x=>req.c[x].exports).filter(x=>x)){if(m.default&&m.default.getToken!==undefined){return console.log('%cYour Token: %c'+m.default.getToken(),'color:gray','color:#5865F2;font-weight:bold;font-size:14px')}if(m.getToken!==undefined){return console.log('%cYour Token: %c'+m.getToken(),'color:gray','color:#5865F2;font-weight:bold;font-size:14px')}}}]);
